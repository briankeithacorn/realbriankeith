# CAO AI Audit — Three Stages, Seven Steps

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Status:** Working draft synthesized from conversation. Hero lines and final naming remain Brian’s.  
**Context:** Incorporates iterative forensics (guided by interviews rather than a single early blanket pull), light preliminary report as client gate, and three higher-level stages containing the seven steps.

---

## Stage 1 — Where Does It Hurt (Steps 1–3)

**Step 1 — Executive Orientation**  
Brian interviews the owner and core leadership to capture the high-level picture, competitive stance, and initial assessment of major functions.

**Step 2 — Expanded Interviews**  
The team interviews additional executives and selected frontline staff to surface day-to-day AI use, early contradictions, and department-level reality.

**Step 3 — Targeted Forensics**  
Function- or department-specific data review (QuickBooks screen-share for AI app spend, app permissions, browser extensions, tool usage exports) guided by what Steps 1–2 revealed.

**Client Deliverable — Preliminary Report**  
Short planning document: one-page executive summary plus one page per identified problem area. Names the top hypotheses, the indications supporting them, and the areas recommended for deeper work. Client confirms or redirects focus. Brief and professional; no exhaustive evidence or full method disclosure.

---

## Stage 2 — Dig Deeper (Steps 4–6)

**Step 4 — Targeted Deep Dive**  
Re-interviews and additional conversations focused on the confirmed problem areas to locate root causes and cross-department conflicts.

**Step 5 — Root-Cause & Culture Analysis**  
Deeper examination of workflows, governance gaps, leadership signals, shadow AI patterns, and cultural friction in the priority areas.

**Step 6 — Path Design**  
Develop specific remediation options (do-it-yourself guides, done-with-you support, and done-for-you builds) tailored to the findings.

**Client Deliverable — Findings & Options Package**  
Detailed synthesis of root causes, supporting evidence, and the three remediation pathways (DIY / DWY / DFY) with scoping notes. Client reviews before final assembly.

---

## Stage 3 — Final Delivery (Step 7)

**Step 7 — Final Report & Live Presentation**  
Compile the Autonomy Map, prioritized recommendations, and 90-day path; present in a live session with the owner.

**Client Deliverable — Final Report + Live Presentation**  
Complete Autonomy Map, prioritized plan, and 90-day path, delivered in a live owner session.

---

## Notes
- Forensics are iterative and guided by interview findings rather than a single early blanket data pull.
- Preliminary report is the explicit client gate between Stage 1 and Stage 2.
- Stage 2 deliverable is the bridge package that enables the final assembly in Stage 3.
- All naming and hero lines remain Brian’s to finalize.
